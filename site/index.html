02 — Private by design

Nothing you say leaves your computer. No account. No cloud. No subscription. A model on your own machine, listening only when you ask — and forgetting the moment you stop.

03 — Features

Small app. Big ears.

Dictate into anything.

Messages, email, your code editor, a browser form. One global shortcut starts listening; each pause becomes a pasted phrase.
